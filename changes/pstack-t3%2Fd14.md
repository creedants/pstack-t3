- kind feature
- `$B dish D1 --usage-limit <provider/model> --role <role> --error <file>|- --catalog <file> --parent <provider/model> [--author ...] [--options <json>]` classifies a provider usage limit with the classifier `roles.py backup` already uses, records one cooldown row in `threads.tsv` for the failed seat and one `cooldown` event in `log.tsv`, and prints exactly one next step: `relaunch on <seat>`, `panel on <seat>, <seat>`, or `parked until <end>`
- the command lives on `$B dish` because `commands/__init__.py` is the only place a top-level subcommand is registered, `$B dish` owns the item, its attempt number, its state, and `threads.tsv`, and `--worker-ended` already sets the precedent of an exclusive dish flag family. `$B model` is restaurant-wide seat health with no dish and no attempt
- a second limit error for the same attempt and role parks the dish and records the event with an empty replacement, and a dish in progress moves to `blocked` while a dish in review stays in review
- the cooldown ends at the reset time the error names, in an ISO date-time, a clock time with `UTC`, `GMT`, or `Z`, or a relative span, and at now plus the new `cooldownMinutes` setting when it names none. `$B set --cooldown-minutes` sets that window, which defaults to 60 and refuses anything under 1
- `$B watch` prints `cooling <provider/model> until <end> (<dish>)` for each live cooldown after the `skipped` lines, every live cooldown provider is passed to `roles.py backup` as `--out`, and a cooldown whose end has passed is not passed and prints nothing
- `$B close` prints a `## Seat cooldowns` section naming each cooldown's dish, seat, start, end, and the seat used instead, and prints no section when the store records none
