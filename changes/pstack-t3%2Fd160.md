- `brigade.py` records a kind and an optional goal on each ticket and work item, and keeps the goals in a new table, `goals.tsv`. `ticket add --summary` needs `--kind`, one of `feature`, `fix`, `polish`, `upkeep`, and `study`, and takes `--goal G<n>` with the id of an open or paused goal. `ticket set --kind`, `--goal`, and `--no-goal` change a waiting ticket and are refused on a ticket in another state and on a waiting ticket that an item in flight names. `goal add` records an open goal with a name, a purpose, and a done test. `goal set` changes a goal, and a move to `done` or to `dropped` needs `--evidence` and is refused while the goal has a waiting ticket, an item in flight, or an assigned ticket in an item that is not under that goal. `goal list` prints each open or paused goal with its counts of waiting tickets, items in flight, merged items, and send-backs, then the counts of unassigned work. `fire` gives the new work item the goal of the first ticket in `--tickets` and that ticket's kind. When that ticket has no kind, the kind is `--kind`, or without it `fix` on station `bug-fix` or `perf-issue` and `study` on station `investigation`, and `fire` is refused on any other station. The first ticket records the kind the item took. A ticket after the first must have the first ticket's goal or no goal unless `--ride-along` names it, and a ticket named there must have priority low and must not be a feature. `ticket add --from-report` gives each ticket the goal of the report's item and, when that item has a kind, the kind `polish`, and it refuses `--kind` and `--goal`. `ticket move` hands the kind to the other coordinator and leaves the goal behind. `ticket list` and `startable` print a ticket's kind and goal after its priority. `status` prints a `kinds:` line and a `goals:` line with the counts of waiting tickets and items in flight under each, and an `untagged:` line with the number of waiting tickets that have no kind. A line with nothing to count is absent. `close` prints the kind on the lines of Merged, In progress, and New tickets, not started, and prints the lines of such a section under one heading per goal when one of them has a goal. A kind cell that holds a value other than one of the five kinds and a goal cell that holds an id with no row in `goals.tsv` read as empty, and `status`, `goal list`, `ticket list`, `startable`, and `close` print a warning for the cell on standard error. `fire` prints it for a ticket it names when it reaches its kind and goal check. `open` writes `goals.tsv` into a new store. A store written before this change reads with every ticket and item unassigned and with no kind. A command that appends a row to `rail.tsv` or `dishes.tsv` there writes the row with the two new cells, and a command that rewrites the table writes every row with them. A `brigade.py` from before this change then calls that table malformed. `activity.py` reads a work item row with or without the two cells and prints the same page for both.
- The prefixes `--no` and `--r` stop working, because `--no-goal` and `--ride-along` made them ambiguous, and the full flag names `--no-decision` and `--reason` work as before.
