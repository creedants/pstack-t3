- `land.py slot` and `land.py slot --exclusive` now take turns through OFD locks on `governor/turn`, a new file beside the slot files. A caller that cannot take its slots takes a numbered ticket and holds a lock on that ticket's byte while it waits, with no slot lock held. A later caller leaves a free slot alone when an earlier ticket that needs the same slots could take its slots at that moment. Worker and landing callers never need the same slot, and an exclusive caller needs every slot. An exclusive ticket cannot take its slots while any slot is busy, so a later caller takes a free slot while an exclusive waits. A ticket lasts only as long as the process that holds its lock, so a killed waiter leaves nothing to delete. The slot files and their `flock` locks are unchanged. A `land.py` from before this change never opens `governor/turn`, so it can take a free slot ahead of a waiting ticket. On a filesystem where the OFD lock fails with `EINVAL` or `ENOTSUP`, `slot` prints `land: this filesystem does not support the slot governor's ordering lock`, exits 1, and does not run the command.
