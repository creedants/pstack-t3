- The brigade skill and the brigade-admin skill show `--kind` on every `ticket add --summary` line. `--kind` is one of `feature`, `fix`, `polish`, `upkeep`, and `study`, and it is required with `--summary`. `--goal` on that line takes an open or paused goal, and a ticket added without it is unassigned. `ticket set --kind`, `--goal`, and `--no-goal` change a waiting ticket. They are refused on a ticket in another state and on a waiting ticket that an item in flight names, and `--goal` there takes an open or paused goal. `goal add` records an open goal and prints its id. `--share` on it is a whole number of 1 or more. A `goal set` move to `done` or `dropped` needs `--evidence` and is refused while a ticket of the goal is waiting, an item of the goal is in progress, in review, passed, sent back, blocked, or waiting to land, or an assigned ticket of the goal is not in an item of the goal. A done or dropped goal moves only to open. `--evidence` on open or paused is refused. `goal list` prints each open or paused goal, and each done or dropped goal that still has a waiting ticket, an item in flight, or a ticket riding along, then the counts of unassigned work. `--all` also prints each other done or dropped goal. A ticket riding along is an assigned ticket of that goal that is not in an item of that goal. `fire` takes `--kind` and `--ride-along`. The item takes the first ticket's goal, and that ticket's kind when it has one. A `--kind` that differs from that kind is refused. `fire` is refused when the first ticket has no kind, no `--kind` is passed, and the station is not `bug-fix`, `perf-issue`, or `investigation`. `ticket add --from-report` takes no `--kind` and no `--goal`. Each ticket takes the goal of the report's item, whatever that goal's state. When that item has a kind, the ticket is `polish`, because a follow-up is filed with no priority and a report ticket with no priority is low. When the item has no kind, the ticket has no kind. `status` prints a kinds line, a goals line, and an untagged line when each has something to count. The guide's list of empty store tables names `goals.tsv`.
