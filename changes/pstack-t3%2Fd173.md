- The HTML work-item strip groups its shown items under matching goal names when its items have a recognized kind or a resolved goal. Each group has one count line of the chips' state words. A kind tag is feature, fix, polish, upkeep, or study. Any other kind cell has no tag. When the strip is grouped, items without a resolved goal share an Unassigned group, last. With no recognized kind and no resolved goal in the strip, the HTML page keeps its previous form. The text form groups the work items it lists when one of those listed items has a recognized kind or a resolved goal. Each text group has one count line of the state words of the items in that group. A listed item with kind feature, fix, polish, upkeep, or study shows that word as a tag. Any other kind cell has no tag. When the text form is grouped, a listed item without a resolved goal is in an Unassigned group, last. When none of the items the text form lists has a recognized kind or a resolved goal, the text form keeps its previous form.
