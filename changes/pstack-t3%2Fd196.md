- `land.py slot --exclusive` stops its command 600 seconds after the hold takes every slot. `--for SECONDS` sets that one hold's cap. A value over 600 needs `--reason`, which is stored on the holder record with control characters removed and cut to 80 characters. The maximum is 7200 seconds. Zero, negative, and non-numeric values are refused. There is no way to turn the cap off. The command runs in its own session. At the cap, land.py sends SIGTERM to that process group and SIGKILL 5 seconds later, prints one stderr line naming the cap and `--for`, and exits 124. A command that finishes sooner keeps its status and output. SIGTERM or Ctrl-C on land.py still stops the group and releases the slots. A waiter names an exclusive hold's cap and its reason. A record with no cap field prints as it does today. Worker slots and the landing slot have no cap. A stderr error while printing a wait line does not stop the waiter from taking a slot. An older installed copy that takes an exclusive hold has no cap. This item cannot change that.
