- The activity page reads `threads.tsv` with the same missing-file and malformed-line handling as its other tables. A thread row has eight cells. Each work item lists the rows for that item that stay on the page. An attempt whose text is ASCII digits and whose length is from 1 to 18 sorts by the integer value of that text. Any other attempt sorts after those by its text. Rows with the same attempt keep their place in the file. A row shows the word attempt, the attempt text, the role, and the seat, dropping the role and the seat when the filter leaves them empty, then `open` or `ended`. A row with an empty end cell is open, and every other row is ended. The page leaves off attempt lines that do not fit in `--max-bytes`, starting from the last in display order, until the output and its newline fit or no attempt line remains. One note counts those lines. It says the page is at its size limit and tells the reader to use a larger `--max-bytes`. The HTML data element carries those phrases under `L` when any item has one, with the same escaping as its other strings, and the plain form prints each phrase under the item. A store with no `threads.tsv`, and a file that has only its header, draw no such lines.
