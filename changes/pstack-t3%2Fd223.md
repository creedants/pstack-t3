- The activity page reads `threads.tsv` with the same missing-file and malformed-line handling as its other tables. A thread row has eight cells. Each work item lists the rows for that item that stay on the page. The rank is the attempt cell as stored. A cell of exactly 1 to 18 ASCII digits sorts by its integer, and any other cell sorts after those by its stored text. Rows with the same rank keep their place in the file. A thread-column reading that holds a character other than a space is private text even when it reads as ordinary words, and the page omits it from both forms, including when the same value sits in the attempt, the role, or the seat. A row shows the word attempt, the attempt text, the role, and the seat, dropping the role and the seat when the filter leaves them empty, then `open` or `ended`. A row with an empty end cell is open, and every other row is ended. The page leaves off attempt lines that do not fit in `--max-bytes`, starting from the last in display order, until the output and its newline fit or no attempt line remains. One note counts those lines. It says the page is at its size limit and tells the reader to use a larger `--max-bytes`. The HTML data element carries those phrases under `L` when any item has one, with the same escaping as its other strings, and the plain form prints each phrase under the item. A store with no `threads.tsv`, and a file that has only its header, draw no such lines.
