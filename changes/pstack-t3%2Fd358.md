- `t3/added/brigade/scripts/activity.py` folds earlier attempts. For each work item not finished per `finished(group)`, ledger lines at the max attempt rank stay current and earlier ranks fold into one summary. The summary counts earlier ledger lines by role in `ROLES` order then unknown sorted plus lane-only labels. State is `all ended`, `all open`, or `N open plus M ended`. A depth-0 row labelled `earlier worker` marks itself and following depth greater than 0 rows earlier until the next depth-0 row, and those rows fold to the same summary and expand on click to the full rows. One distinct rank and no earlier-worker family keeps all lines current with an empty fold.
- `t3/added/brigade/scripts/activity.py --live-page` takes an optional `ORIGIN`. With no value it polls `http://127.0.0.1:48731`. With a value it polls `ORIGIN`. It accepts only `http://` or `https://` origins with no userinfo, path, query, or fragment. It refuses anything else with `activity: --live-page takes an http:// or https:// origin with no userinfo, path, query, or fragment; pass one such as https://home.example.ts.net:48731, or omit it to use http://127.0.0.1:48731`.
- `tests/test_activity.py` covers an item with three attempts showing current rows, one count line with per-role counts, and full rows for expansion. It covers a single-attempt item rendering with no fold. It covers `--live-page` with a valid `https` origin, the default, and refusal of an origin with a path, a query, and a non-`http` scheme. It locks `ROLES` to the store role tuple.
