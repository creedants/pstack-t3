- `tests/test_activity_render.py` builds the probe's flags with `headless_flags`, which returns `--headless` alone on macOS and `--headless --disable-gpu --no-sandbox` on every other platform. Chrome's headless command-line reference documents `--headless` alone with `--dump-dom` and with `--screenshot --window-size`, and passes neither `--disable-gpu` nor `--no-sandbox` (https://developer.chrome.com/docs/automation-and-testing/headless-cli).
- `select_browser` appends the first three non-empty lines of the browser's stderr, through `stderr_segment`, to a probe's failure text after a nonzero exit, when the browser wrote to stderr. A browser that writes nothing to stderr keeps the failure text it had.
- `tests/test_activity_render.py` adds `test_macos_probe_omits_disable_gpu_and_no_sandbox`, which pins the flags between the browser and the profile on macOS and on every other platform, and `test_probe_failure_carries_the_browser_stderr`, which pins the stderr lines in the skip segment.
