- `pass record` does not read a line of a report's `## Threads` section that opens a fence after up to three spaces, nor a line until that fence closes. It skips a non-blank line it reads that is plain prose, that is when it starts after any leading whitespace with neither a list marker nor a `#`, holds no backticked thread id, and names no lowercase child role, and refuses by its number every other non-blank line it reads that is not one item of the form - <role> `<thread id>` <seat>. A list marker is a hyphen, an asterisk, a plus, a bullet, or a number followed by a dot or a closing parenthesis. A backticked thread id is a backticked value holding a colon and holding no whitespace.
