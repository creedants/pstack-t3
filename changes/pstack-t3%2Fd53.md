- Each intake source has one owning brigade coordinator. `brigade.py open --intake` and `set --intake` record it in `restaurant.json` and refuse a source a sibling owns. `ticket add` refuses a source this coordinator does not own and a ref that is still live here or in a sibling, following moved tickets to the end of their chain. `ticket move <id> --to <sibling>` hands a waiting ticket to a sibling through its `inbox/`, and `ticket take` files it once, keyed by handoff id, and finishes a take a crash interrupted. Every write holds a lock on `restaurant.lock`, an append drops a killed append's unfinished tail before it writes, and a table line that does not parse stops the command with `brigade: <table> line <n> is malformed; fix or remove it`. `watch`, `status`, and `close` show handoffs until they are taken.
