- `land.py` carries out the executive admin's rulings. `lease reserve --for <prefix> --paths ... --ruling R<n>` holds paths for one coordinator. Every admission refuses another holder's overlap with `paths reserved for docs/ by ruling R4 until <time>`. The reservation arms only when no other holder's live lease overlaps and the cap and share have room, counts as one change in flight until the winner claims from it, shrinks as the winner claims, and expires 2 hours after it arms. `lease unreserve S<n>` lifts one, and `lease list` and `lease check` show them. `share --for <prefix> <n>` limits a prefix's changes in flight with `docs/ is at its share: 2 of 2` and refuses shares that add up to more than the cap. `contest --holders <a>,<b>` holds both holders' entries out of every batch and every PR opening, `contest --settle C<n> --first <holder>` lets the first land before the other, `--settle` refuses an order that closes a cycle, and `contest --cancel` lifts the hold. Contests take the queue lock. Every ruling write requires `--owner` and refuses a generation below its prefix's floor. `init` with `--merge-method` in any mode, `mode --merge-method` in any mode, and `mode merge` now check the method against the repository's allowed methods, and `mode merge` swaps a disallowed stored method for an allowed one. In merge mode, a PR whose checks failed and whose auto-merge `gh pr merge --disable-auto` cannot turn off now pauses the queue and stays awaiting merge instead of bouncing, because it can still merge on its own. `contest --settle` keeps the order once the first holder has an entry landing or awaiting merge.
