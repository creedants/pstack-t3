- `worktree-audit.sh` marks `T3=yes` for a worktree under `<T3 home>/worktrees` and under `worktreesDirectory` or `previousWorktreesDirectories` in `<T3 home>/userdata/settings.json`. `--t3-worktrees` or `T3_WORKTREES` replaces the configured directory. An empty, relative, or filesystem-root setting keeps the old default. `t3/runtime.md` names `preview_hover`, `preview_drag`, `preview_select`, `preview_upload`, and `preview_dialog`, and says when to use each.
