- brigade's skill teaches the executive admin end to end: opening it, its services, its messages, the ruling rules and what it escalates, what it never does, recovery and retirement, and what the user hears. A coordinator with `reportsTo` sends its events to the admin, acts on its requests through `inbox take` and `inbox done`, carries out rulings and may appeal, opens contests, and keeps an hourly service. `brigade.py sync` never moves a cursor back when an older sync finishes after a newer one. On a send-back or a queue bounce the coordinator finishes with the old worker before the dish re-enters progress, so `hang` still records an open run. `watch` now prints `reported Nm ago, not in review; read the thread`.
