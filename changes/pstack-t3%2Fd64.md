- brigade's skill teaches the executive admin end to end: opening it, its services, its messages, the ruling rules and what it escalates, what it never does, recovery and retirement, and what the user hears. The admin escalates only when no rule separates the parties, records the user's answer to its own questions with `86 answer`, and passes a coordinator's reply, including a decline, to the user. The skill names which coordinator lines leave a store row that `sync` relays and which are message-only, so the admin acts on a message-only line from the message that woke it, and a coordinator whose send of one fails tells the user at every level. A coordinator with `reportsTo` sends its events to the admin, acts on its requests through `inbox take` and `inbox done`, carries out rulings and may appeal, opens contests, and keeps an hourly service. `brigade.py sync` never moves a cursor back when an older sync finishes after a newer one. On a send-back or a queue bounce the coordinator finishes with the old worker before the dish re-enters progress, so `hang` still records an open run. `watch` now prints `reported Nm ago, not in review; read the thread (<where>)`.
