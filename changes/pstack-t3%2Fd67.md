- kind feature
- `ticket age` moves waiting tickets whose priority cell is `low` and whose kind is polish or upkeep to `notes.tsv` when they were filed more than the limit ago, then drops them. The limit sits in `restaurant.json` under `ticketAge`, defaults to 14 days, is set with `set --ticket-age N`, and 0 or `""` turns aging off. The evening wake runs `ticket age` before the report cue whenever aging is on, and the evening report gains a `## Waiting list` section whenever a waiting ticket is on record or a ticket aged out since the last report.
