- brigade: a worker brief says the timebox orders the work and never waives a playbook step (How, Architect, investigation, or the implementation delegate). At the limit the worker writes the report with what remains instead of skipping steps. The skill says the same when it sizes a timebox and when it reviews a report. The coordinator checks that the report names what remains. An unexplained skip, including one cited to the timebox, is a send-back.
- brigade: returning a sent-back or bounced item to in progress clears its worker thread. `dish --thread` refuses a thread an earlier attempt of that item already recorded. `watch` names an in-progress item with no worker thread, and the liveness check launches a fresh worker for that line. A send-back launches a fresh worker with `t3_thread_launch` and does not call `t3_thread_send` on the old worker.
