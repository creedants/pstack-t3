- A worker no longer sits idle behind a child that never finishes. The runtime tells a thread that nothing else wakes to keep its turn while a child is open, wait on it with `t3_thread_wait` in 5-minute steps, and treat a child with no new activity for 10 minutes as stalled. It takes a stalled child's written result and cancels the child, or cancels it and respawns it once. Children omit `runtimeMode`, because a child lowered to `approval-required` waits on an approval no tool can answer. Brigade's liveness check reads the thread of each running worker and nudges one that has been idle for over 10 minutes with no report while its timebox is open.
