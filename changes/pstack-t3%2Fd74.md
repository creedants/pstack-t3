- `docs/light-mode.md` designs a light mode, one `full` or `light` setting that cuts the sub-agents and reviewers around each change while keeping tests, the build gate, the landing queue's checks, a review by another model family at every head SHA, and the owner fences. It includes a census of every spawn point with a light policy for each, one effective-mode input that `roles.py show` and `roles.py mode` share, a gate review for standalone playbooks that reuses only the latest cross-family pass at the exact head SHA while full mode keeps its patch-id policy, waivers keyed by playbook and attempt kind, escalation rules with exact path semantics and durable state, reproducible measurement commands, and an ordered build plan.
