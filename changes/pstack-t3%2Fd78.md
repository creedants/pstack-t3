- `roles.py check-brief` now checks a brief that has a `Mode:` line. The line's whole value must be `full` or `light`. `roles.py check-brief` rejects a second `Mode`, `Mode source`, `Attempt`, `Waived by mode`, or `Gate` line. A light brief needs `Attempt:` set to `first`, `fix`, or `bounce`. The `Waived by mode:` line must match `LIGHT_WAIVERS` for that playbook and attempt. An empty waiver means the brief has no `Waived by mode:` line. Under `Mode: full`, the brief has no `Waived by mode:` line. A brief with no `Mode:` line checks as before. When the brief has a `Mode:` line, the runtime tells a parent to copy that line's bare value into `roles.py show --brief-mode` and to pass no other mode flag. In a light session with no `Mode:` line, the parent passes `--session-mode light`.
