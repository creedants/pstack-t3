- brigade carries the light or full mode. `open --mode` and `set --mode` record the restaurant's mode, and `brief` writes the item's `Mode:` lines from `roles.py mode` with `Gate: brigade`. A lease over an `escalate` path, a second send-back, `fire --mode full`, or `dish --mode full` moves the item to full once, as a `mode` row in `log.tsv`. A light worker's `Contested:` report gets a fresh full worker. `pass check --json` prints `crossFamily`, so a standalone light gate can reuse a brigade pass at the same head. The coordinator confirms the resolved options reached the worker and the verifier. An open decision for an item holds it, so `watch` launches no worker or verifier for it until the decision is answered. An item decision closes only when the answer matches a listed option other than its default, so an answer of `keep parked` leaves the item held.
