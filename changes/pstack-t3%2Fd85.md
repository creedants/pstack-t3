- `roles.py check-brief` now requires the whole printed `roles.py mode` block directly under the persona body, not only its `Playbook:` and `Mode:` lines. It refuses task prose between `Mode:` and a required `Attempt:` or `Waived by mode:` line, and a task line formatted as `Gate: ...` before the block. A `Gate:` line in the task text after the complete block still passes, because tasks legally name gates. Tests in `tests/roles_tests/test_check_brief.py` pin prose before `Attempt:`, prose before `Waived by mode:`, an early `Gate:` decoy, and the legal task `Gate:`.
