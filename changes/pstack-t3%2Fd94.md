- The runtime documents five more T3 tools. A separate thread the user asked for can start from another thread with `t3_thread_fork`, and `t3_thread_merge_back` carries its reasoning to a related thread when the user authorizes it. Child work, Orchestrate sub-coordinators, and review rounds stay child tasks. `t3_thread_configure` moves an idle launched owner to a resolved seat when a playbook step calls for another model, and the owner keeps its gates, scope, and retry count. `t3_queue_promote_to_steer` delivers a correction already queued to an owner into its active run, and it counts as delivered only when the recipient's activity shows it. `run_scheduled_task_now` dispatches an enabled interval schedule's ready work now and reports dispatch, not completion. A reply already due can publish its tables as a page through `html_preview` and `html_render`, and brigade at digest renders no page. Orchestrate links the configure rule from its tool-error retry and the page rule from its Reply.
- `no-comments` does not run under a read-only instruction, because it edits files and delegates edits. The caller reports comment findings in its own review.
